## Description
Skipping Counter turns your iPhone into a jump-rope coach. Prop it on the floor
a few metres away, start skipping, and it counts every jump through the camera.

• Counts skips automatically. On-device body tracking follows your bounce;
  walking around, standing or stretching aren't counted.
• Real training time. The timer runs only while you're skipping, so rests
  don't inflate your workout.
• Double unders counted separately.
• Live cadence in skips per minute.
• Big numbers you can read from across the room, and a glowing border that
  shows at a glance whether you're being counted.
• Private by design. Video is analysed on your iPhone and never leaves it.
  No account, no ads, no tracking. Works offline.

Works with every style: basic bounce, alternate foot, high knees, criss-cross
and double unders.

## App Review information
- Sign-in required: **No**
- Notes for the reviewer:

  > Skipping Counter counts jump-rope skips using the front camera and an
  > on-device pose model (MediaPipe), bundled with the app; it works offline
  > and sends no data anywhere. To try it: tap Start, allow the camera, place
  > the iPhone on the floor or a shelf 2–4 m away so your whole body is visible,
  > and jump in place (a rope is optional for review). The count increases
  > after the first few jumps; walking in front of the camera is not counted.
