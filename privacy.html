Privacy Policy

Skipping Counter · Last updated 9 November 2026

Short version: Skipping Counter does not collect, store or share any personal data. Everything happens on your device.

Camera

The app uses your device's camera to watch you skip and count your jumps. Each video frame is analysed on your device by a body-pose model that runs inside the app, and is then discarded. No images or video are uploaded, saved or sent anywhere.

Data we collect

None. The app has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking and no third-party SDKs that collect data. Your skip counts and training times exist only on your screen while the app is open.

Network access

The app works offline. The web version at this address downloads the app's own files from our hosting provider (Google Firebase Hosting), which, like any web server, may keep standard technical logs such as IP addresses for security and operations. We do not use those logs to identify you.

Children

The app collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
