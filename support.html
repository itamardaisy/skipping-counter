Skipping Counter — Support

Counts your jump-rope skips and real training time through the camera.

Getting a good count

  • Prop the phone on the floor 2–4 metres away, angled up.
  • Keep your whole body in the frame, head to feet, with some space above your head.
  • Good, even lighting helps; avoid standing with a bright window behind you.
  • The border glows green while skips are being counted, amber when it sees you but you're not skipping, and red when it can't see you.
  • The timer only runs while you are actually skipping, so rests don't count.

Double unders

Double unders are counted separately. Start a set with a few ordinary jumps so the app can learn your normal jump height.

Camera permission

If you declined camera access, enable it in Settings → Skipping Counter → Camera. Video never leaves your phone — see the privacy policy.
